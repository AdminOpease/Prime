# Loom walkthrough script (for Ozan to record)

Target length: **4-5 minutes**. This is what Eduard watches once when he
first gets access. Record it in a single take — don't over-polish.

**Before recording**:
- Have `https://primebodywork.sanity.studio` and `https://primebodywork.co.uk`
  open in separate tabs
- Have a phone photo handy to demonstrate an image upload
- Have loom.com/record open, screen + audio (webcam optional but nice)

---

## Section 1 — 30 seconds — What this is

> "Hey Eduard, welcome to your new website. I'm going to spend about five
> minutes showing you how to update the content whenever you need to. You
> don't need to remember any of this — it'll all be in a cheatsheet I've
> sent you."

Show the live website (`primebodywork.co.uk`), scroll through the homepage
briefly so he sees the layout.

## Section 2 — 30 seconds — Where you edit

> "To edit anything, you go here — `primebodywork.sanity.studio`. This is
> your admin panel. Bookmark it and pin the tab."

Switch to the Studio tab, show the sidebar with Site Settings / Homepage /
Services / Gallery / Testimonials / Accreditations / Pages.

## Section 3 — 1 minute — The most common edit: opening hours

> "The thing you'll change most is probably opening hours. Click Site
> Settings, click the Opening Hours tab, change the times, and hit Publish
> at the bottom."

**Do it live**: click Site Settings → Opening Hours → change Monday from
08:00 to 08:30 → click Publish → wait 2 seconds.

> "That's it. Changes appear on the live website within about a minute."

Switch back to the live site tab, refresh, briefly point at the footer
where the hours update.

## Section 4 — 1 minute — Adding a service

> "New service is just as easy. Services → plus Create."

**Do it live**: create a new fake service (delete it before publishing —
or if you accidentally publish, we clean it up after).

- Title: "Alloy Wheel Refurbishment"
- Category: pick Bodywork
- Short description: one sentence
- Show that you can add a hero image (drag a phone photo in)
- Click Publish

Switch to live site → /services → show the new card appears.

## Section 5 — 1 minute — Adding a before/after gallery photo

> "The gallery is where you show off your work. Every job is a chance to
> add a photo."

**Do it live**: Gallery → Create → give it a title → drop in two phone
photos (before/after) → Publish → show on the site.

## Section 6 — 30 seconds — Where messages go

> "When someone fills in the contact form, you'll get an email at
> eduard@primebodywork.co.uk. It has all their details and any photos they
> uploaded, and you can just reply to it directly to email them back."

Show a screenshot of the email format if you have one (or skip and describe).

## Section 7 — 30 seconds — Closing

> "That's really it. The cheatsheet I sent you has step-by-step for
> everything else — logo, phone number, address, testimonials, and so on.
> If something breaks or you're not sure, just message me."

---

## Reminders while recording

- **Don't apologise** for any UI quirks — they're not bugs, they're normal
- **Slow down on the "Publish" button** — that's the one thing he needs
  to remember to click every time
- **Skip the fancy stuff** — don't show him GROQ queries, previews, Vision
  tool. He doesn't need any of it.
- **Keep energy up** — a 4-min video with dead air feels like 10
